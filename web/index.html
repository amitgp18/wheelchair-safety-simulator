    Move requests

    Each press adds a request to the queue. Arrow keys or W A S D add moves, B adds BACK, N processes one request.

    Process the queue

    Auto-run takes one request from the queue every 0.6 seconds. Turn it off to step by hand and watch the queue fill up.

    Room and simulation

    Turn on Edit obstacles, then select a floor cell to place or remove an obstacle. With the keyboard, Tab into the room, move with the arrow keys and press Enter. Reset keeps your obstacles. Default map restores the original room.

        Recovery and revalidation

          How the stop works

          • The stop is not a queue request. It acts the moment it is pressed, even with eight requests waiting.
          • All waiting requests are removed and are never resumed on their own.
          • New requests and processing are refused until recovery is confirmed.
          • The history stack is kept, so BACK still works after recovery.
          • Recovery needs your confirmation and four passing checks.
